I’m Adinan Apparambil, a sports physiotherapist with over seven years of professional experience across sports physiotherapy, physical rehabilitation, musculoskeletal care, and neurological rehabilitation.

My experience spans sports settings, rehabilitation centres, homecare, and hospital environments in the UAE and India. I work with a focus on understanding movement, supporting recovery, and helping people build confidence as they return to their everyday activities and sport.

My approach brings together individual assessment, appropriate rehabilitation techniques, and exercise-based progression—always with the person’s needs and goals at the centre.
